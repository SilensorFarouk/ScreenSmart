English

Introduction: Welcome! In this tutorial, I will show you how to take and save a screenshot on a Windows computer.

Step 1: First, click the search {% blocktrans with = trimmed %}{% endblocktrans %} and search for Snipping Tool. Open the application.

Step 2: Click the New button to begin taking a screenshot.

Step 3: Drag your mouse over the area of the screen you want to capture.

Step 4: Click the Save button, choose a location and enter a file name.

Step 5: Navigate to the location where you saved the screenshot and open it.

Conclusion: Congratulations! You now know how to take and save a screenshot on Windows.
